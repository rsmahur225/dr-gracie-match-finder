WHATS INSIDE

what's in it and why it matters.

Nano-Hydroxyapatite

nHA · 10% in toothpaste

A naturally occurring mineral that makes up 97% of tooth enamel. nHA particles bond to the tooth surface, filling micro-cracks and supporting enamel health. The gold standard in Japanese oral care since the 1980s.

Core active ingredient

Zinc Citrate

0.5–1% · max in Gum Reset

Supports a balanced oral environment and freshness from the source. Included at maximum concentration in the Gum Reset set — not just masking, but addressing the underlying cause.

Gum & breath support

Potassium Nitrate

5% · Barrier Repair focus

Calms nerve sensitivity by blocking pain signal pathways in dentinal tubules. Included at full 5% in the Barrier Repair set — dialed for those with sensitivity concerns.

Sensitivity comfort

Sodium Hyaluronate

0.1% · in Mouth Serum

The same ingredient in your skincare serum — now for your oral mucosa. Supports mucosal hydration overnight in the leave-on Calm Mint Serum. Think skincare serum, for your enamel.
