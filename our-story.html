OUR STORY

Twenty years of chair-side notes, finally in a tube.

Dr. Gracie isn't a brand built in a marketing room. It's twenty years of patient conversations, clinical patterns, and one stubborn belief: oral care shouldn't make you feel embarrassed.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Oral care has spent decades telling people they're failing at a routine. We think the routine was failing them.

Generic toothpastes formulated for everyone treat no one specifically. People with bleeding gums use the same tube as people chasing brighter teeth. Patients blame themselves for not flossing harder, for not brushing longer — when the product they're holding wasn't built for the problem they actually have.

We started Dr. Gracie to fix that exact gap. Three concern-specific routines, dosed at concentrations that match the problem. Built from clinical patterns Dr. Gracie has seen daily for two decades, not from focus-group trends.

THE JOURNEY

From a single clinic in the Rio Grande Valley
to a tube with her name on it.

  1. 2003

    The first chair

    Dr. Gracie opens her first orthodontic practice in the Rio Grande Valley. The waiting list fills within six months.

  2. 2012

    The pattern

    After nearly a decade in practice, she notices the same three patient stories repeating: dull teeth, bleeding gums, sharp sensitivity. The products on the shelf weren't matched to any of them.

  3. 2019

    The first formula

    She starts hand-mixing concentrations for specific patients. The nano-hydroxyapatite trials begin. The leave-on serum prototype takes its first form.

  4. 2024

    The brand

    Dr. Gracie incorporates. The packaging — tin tubes, numbered routines, signature script — comes from her own handwriting on patient notes.

  5. 2026

    Launch

    Three Match Sets enter early access. First 500 customers get the founder note in the box and 15% locked in for life.
