WHAT'S NOT IN IT

The shortlist of things we left out — on purpose.

We don't shame other brands. But here's what didn't make it into our formula, and the brief reason why.

  • ✕

    Fluoride

    nHA achieves comparable remineralization in clinical comparisons, without the toxicity ceiling for children.

  • ✕

    SLS (Sodium Lauryl Sulfate)

    The foaming agent linked to mouth ulcers and dry mouth. Doesn't clean better — just feels foamier.

  • ✕

    Peroxide

    Aggressive whitening accelerates enamel damage and sensitivity. We chose gradual nHA tone-lift instead.

  • ✕

    Triclosan & Parabens

    Phased out of personal care for endocrine and microbiome concerns. No place in something you put in your mouth twice a day.

  • ✕

    Artificial dyes

    Pure aesthetic — we'd rather have a clean ingredient deck.

  • ✕

    High abrasivity

    RDA under 70 across our toothpastes. Whitening claims often rely on harsh abrasives that strip enamel.

FAQS

The questions you probably
want answered first.

Is nano-hydroxyapatite as effective as fluoride?

In peer-reviewed studies (Tschoppe 2011, Amaechi 2019, Kensche 2017) 10% nHA shows remineralization outcomes comparable to fluoride at therapeutic doses — without the systemic toxicity ceiling. We chose it for that reason, not against fluoride.

Will my teeth get whiter? How fast?

Most users report a visible tone shift in 4–6 weeks of consistent twice-daily use. We don't promise overnight results because peroxide-free brightening is gradual by design — we trade speed for enamel preservation.

Is the serum safe to swallow accidentally?

Every ingredient in the Calm Mint Mouth Serum is food-grade or oral-safe at the concentrations used. Small accidental amounts are fine. You should still spit, not rinse, after use.

Are your products safe for kids?

Our adult toothpaste is safe from age 6+. We're developing a kids' line with adjusted abrasivity and flavor — launching late 2026.

Why are concentrations different between sets?

Because the concern is different. Zinc citrate runs higher in Gum Reset; potassium nitrate runs higher in Barrier Repair. We dose actives to the problem, not as marketing window-dressing.
