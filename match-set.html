YOUR ROUTINE

Two minutes morning. Three minutes night.

Designed so it doesn't feel like work. Most users see a visible tone shift in 4–6 weeks of consistent use.

  1. 01

    Morning — Clean (2 min)

    Pea-sized Soft Mint Milk on an ultra-soft brush. Two minutes, gentle pressure, gum-line focus.

  2. 02

    Night — Clean (2 min)

    Same brushing routine before bed. Spit, don't rinse — let the nHA stay on enamel.

  3. 03

    Night — Serum (1 min)

    4–5 drops of Calm Mint Serum, swish for 60 seconds, spit. Do not rinse, do not eat or drink.
