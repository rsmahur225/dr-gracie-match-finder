What's Inside

We will tell you exactly
what's in it and why it matters.

nHA
Nano-Hydroxyapatite nHA 10% in toothpaste

A naturally occurring mineral that makes up 97% of tooth enamel. nHA particles bond to the tooth surface, filling micro-cracks and supporting enamel health. The gold standard in Japanese oral care since the 1980s.

Core active ingredient
ZnC
Zinc Citrate 0.5–1% max in Gum Reset

Supports a balanced oral environment and freshness from the source. Included at maximum concentration in the Gum Reset set — not just masking, but addressing the underlying tissue environment.

Gum & breath support
KNO
Potassium Nitrate 5% Barrier Repair focus

Calms nerve sensitivity by blocking pain signal pathways in dentinal tubules. Included at full 5% in the Barrier Repair set — ideal for those with sensitivity concerns.

Sensitivity comfort
SH
Sodium Hyaluronate 0.1% in Mouth Serum

The same ingredient in your skincare serum — now for your oral mucosa. Supports mucosal hydration overnight in the leave-in Calm Skin Serum. Think skincare-serum, for your tissues.
