Enamel

Enamel cannot regenerate — but it can remineralise.

Your enamel is the hardest substance your body produces. Once it is physically lost, it is gone permanently. However, early-stage enamel erosion — microfractures, softening, early white spots — can be reversed through remineralisation. That process requires the right mineral substrate delivered at the right time.

Hydroxyapatite

The mineral your enamel is actually made of.

96% of enamel is hydroxyapatite — a calcium phosphate crystal. Nano-hydroxyapatite (nHAp) is a synthetic replica of this mineral. When applied in toothpaste form, nHAp particles integrate into the enamel surface and fill microscopic defects. Multiple peer-reviewed studies now show nHAp is as effective as fluoride for remineralisation — without the systemic concerns.

The Microbiome

Your mouth contains over 700 bacterial species.

The oral microbiome is not your enemy. Beneficial bacteria compete with pathogenic strains, regulate pH, and produce compounds that protect gum tissue. Alcohol-based mouthwashes and many antimicrobial agents disrupt this balance indiscriminately. Our formulas are designed to support microbial balance, not destroy it.

Timing

When you use oral care products matters as much as which ones you use.

Saliva flow decreases by up to 70% during sleep — the period of lowest natural enamel protection. Applying remineralising toothpaste at night allows nHAp to remain in contact with enamel during this critical window. Morning brushing serves a different purpose: mechanical cleaning and microbiome reset.

Ingredient Focus

Nano-Hydroxyapatite:
The full picture.

Originally developed by NASA for treating astronaut bone density loss in zero gravity, nHAp has been used in Japanese dentistry since the 1980s. Western oral care is only now catching up.

Origin

Bioidentical to the calcium phosphate in human enamel and bone. Synthesised to match the body's own mineral structure.

Mechanism

Particles of 20–80nm integrate directly into enamel crystalline structure. Works by structural incorporation, not surface coating.

Evidence

Multiple RCTs show equivalence to fluoride for caries prevention. Superior results in dentinal hypersensitivity reduction.

Safety

Biocompatible, non-toxic, non-systemic. No fluorosis risk. Safe for use in children and during pregnancy.
