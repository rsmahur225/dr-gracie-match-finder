Step 1 — Prep

Calm Mint Serum

Apply 2–3 pumps along the gumline before brushing. The alcohol-free formula reduces gingival inflammation and conditions the mucosal tissue, preparing it to respond to the remineralising treatment that follows. Gum tissue absorbs the peptide complex most effectively before mechanical disruption from brushing.

19ml · Nightly · Alcohol-Free

02

Step 2 — Remineralise

Soft Mint Milk Toothpaste

Apply a full-length strip to a soft or sonic toothbrush. Brush for two full minutes, ensuring contact with all surfaces. Do not rinse after spitting — allow the nHAp residue to remain on enamel. The remineralisation process continues for up to 30 minutes post-brushing.

78ml · Nightly · nHAp-Based · Do Not Rinse

03

Step 3 — Colonise

Oral Probiotic Tablet

Place one tablet on the tongue after brushing. Allow it to dissolve slowly over 3–5 minutes. Do not swallow immediately. The probiotic strains require contact with oral mucosa to adhere and colonise. They work most effectively during sleep, when competing microbial activity is lower.

30 Tablets · Dissolve Slowly · Do Not Eat After
